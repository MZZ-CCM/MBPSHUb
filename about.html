About MBPS

Property sourcing built around the investor.

Why MBPS exists, who it's for, and what makes a deal worth bringing to you in the first place.

100%
Off-market access

Moyo Bespoke Property Solutions exists for one purpose: to make UK property investment feel effortless — without sacrificing returns or sleep.

Every brief starts with a private consultation. We profile your goals, capital position, risk appetite and timeline, then go to market with a brief no estate agent will ever see. Most of our deals close before they ever appear on Rightmove.

We're a team — a dedicated client-support function backed by a panel of independent solicitors, mortgage and finance brokers, accountants and trusted contractors. That means whatever your deal needs, the right specialist is already on hand. We work across single-let Buy-to-Let, professional HMO, Serviced Accommodation, commercial conversions and rent-to-rent agreements.

What "effortless" actually means

It doesn't mean passive — every deal still needs your sign-off, your capital, your decision. It means you're never the one chasing a solicitor for an update, never the one trying to work out if a yield projection is realistic, and never finding out about a problem with the property after you've exchanged. We absorb the parts of buying that have nothing to do with whether the investment is actually good.

Why most of it happens before a listing exists

A property that's already on Rightmove has, by definition, been seen by everyone — every agent, every other investor, every cash buyer with an alert set up. The price reflects that competition. The deals worth having are usually found before that competition exists: a landlord retiring, a developer clearing the last few units, an estate going through probate. That's where our actual work happens.
