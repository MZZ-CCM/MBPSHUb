01 About MBPS

We started MBPS to make UK property investment clearer — with the risks set out as plainly as the returns.

Property sourcing built around the investor.

Every brief starts with a private conversation about your goals, capital, risk appetite and timeline. We then search to that brief — including off-market and direct-to-vendor opportunities that aren't widely advertised — and only bring you deals that match it.

Behind that sits a client-support team and a panel of independent solicitors, mortgage and finance brokers, accountants and contractors, so the right specialist can be brought in when your deal needs one. We work across single-let Buy-to-Let, HMO, Serviced Accommodation, commercial conversions and rent-to-rent.

We're probably not the right fit if you're looking for guaranteed returns or a deal by next week. Good property takes time to find, and we'd rather say so up front.

02 What We Do

Five investment strategies, one curated experience.

Every strategy we support is one we know well — from underwriting and refurbishment costs through to tenant management and exit. We won't source what we wouldn't buy ourselves.

Yields shown are indicative gross targets, before costs — not guarantees. Every deal pack sets out the net figures and the assumptions behind them.

Room-by-room lets to professionals or students. Higher potential gross yields, but more management and compliance — including licensing and Article 4 checks, which we do before a deal reaches you.

03 Strategy 03 · Cash flow

Serviced AccommodationSA

Short-stay lets in business and leisure locations. Cash flow can be strong when location, finish and channel management line up — and weak when they don't, so we model occupancy and nightly rate conservatively before you commit.

Typical entry
£150k – £400k
Target gross yield

04 Strategy 04 · Value-add

Commercial & Mixed-Use

Commercial-to-residential conversions, mixed-use stock and small blocks. More complexity and longer timelines, with the potential for higher returns — best for investors with patience and finance in place.

05 Strategy 05 · Low capital

Rent to RentR2R

Lease a property from a landlord on a company let, operate it as a multi-let or serviced asset, and keep the difference. Lower capital outlay and cash flow can start sooner — a common entry point before committing purchase capital. We check landlord consent, the agreement and local council rules, and model the operating numbers so both sides know where they stand.

03 How We Work

A clear path, from brief to completion.

Sourcing is part craft, part discipline. Here's exactly how we engage from the first introduction.

01/ 05
  1. STEP 01

    Discovery Call

    A free 30-minute conversation about your goals, capital, risk appetite and timeline. No fee and no obligation — if we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.

  2. STEP 02

    Onboarding

    If you'd like to go ahead, you sign the Deal Sourcing Agreement and pay a £350 engagement deposit. Your brief is agreed in writing, and our sourcing fee is only due when we secure a deal that matches it.

  3. STEP 03

    Sourcing

    We search to your brief across off-market, direct-to-vendor and agent channels. You only see the deals that match what you've asked for.

  4. STEP 04

    Deal Pack

    Each opportunity comes with financial modelling, comparables, refurbishment costing and exit options — plus the risks and assumptions behind the numbers.

  5. STEP 05

    Completion

    We coordinate the solicitors, broker, surveyor and contractors through to completion — and we're still here once you have the keys.
