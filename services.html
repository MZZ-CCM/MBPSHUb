What We Do

Five investment strategies,
one curated experience.

Every strategy we support is one we know intimately — from underwriting and refurbishment costs through to tenant management and exit. We won't source what we wouldn't buy ourselves.

01

Buy-to-Let (BTL)

Single-let acquisitions selected for strong rental coverage, capital growth potential and minimal management overhead. The foundation of every balanced UK portfolio.

Typical entry£100k – £400k
Target gross yield6% – 9%
02

Houses in Multiple Occupation (HMO)

Room-by-room let to professionals or students. Higher gross yields, more intensive management — best suited to investors comfortable with operations or willing to fund a managed solution.

Typical entry£180k – £500k
Target gross yield10% – 15%
03

Serviced Accommodation (SA)

Short-stay lettings in business and leisure-led locations. Strong cash flow when location, finish and channel management align. We model occupancy and ANR before you commit a penny.

Typical entry£150k – £400k
Target gross yield12% – 20%
04

Commercial & Mixed-Use

Commercial-to-residential conversions, mixed-use stock and small block opportunities. Higher complexity, materially better returns — for investors with patience and finance in place.

Typical entry£250k+
Target gross yield10% – 18%
05

Rent to Rent (R2R)

Lease from a landlord, operate the property as a serviced or multi-let asset, and keep the spread. Lower capital outlay, faster cashflow — the right entry strategy for investors building their first £500–£1,000 per month before they commit purchase capital. We source compliant landlord agreements, model the operating numbers, and structure the company let so both sides are protected.

Typical setup£3k – £8k
Target monthly net£500 – £1,500

Choosing between them

Which strategy is right for you?

Most first-time clients arrive with a strategy already in mind and leave with a different one — usually because the trade-off between yield, management effort and capital required wasn't what they expected on paper. A 15% yield HMO sounds better than a 7% BTL until you account for what running it actually involves; equally, R2R looks like the obvious low-capital entry point right up until a landlord agreement falls through and you've nothing to show for the setup cost.

This is exactly what the Discovery Call in our process is for — matching the strategy to your actual capital, time and risk appetite, not the other way around.
