Our Team & Network

More than a sourcer. A full bench behind every deal.

MBPS pairs an in-house sourcing and client-support team with a vetted panel of independent professionals. You get one point of contact and a whole bench of specialists working in the background.

Founder & Managing Director

Carean Moyo

Leads sourcing & client strategy · MBPS

Carean founded Moyo Bespoke Property Solutions in October 2024 and leads the sourcing and client-strategy side of the business. With three years in property — from underwriting and refurbishment costing through to direct-to-vendor sourcing — he sets the standard every deal pack has to meet before it reaches an investor.

Around him sits a growing team: a client-support function that keeps you updated at every stage, and a panel of independent solicitors, mortgage brokers, accountants and contractors who plug in exactly when a deal needs them. Based in Cardiff, the firm has sourced over £1.5 million in property across the UK.
