In more detail

What each step actually involves.

01 — Discovery Call

Before any sourcing happens, we need to know what "good" looks like for you specifically — budget ceiling, target yield, geography, hands-on or hands-off, and how soon you need to deploy capital. This call is free and commits you to nothing; most clients find it clarifies their own brief as much as it informs ours.

02 — Onboarding

Once we agree to work together, the Deal Sourcing Agreement sets out our fee structure and scope in writing, and the engagement deposit confirms you're a live, funded mandate rather than a speculative enquiry. This is also when your investor profile — strategy, budget, location — gets locked in and used to score every candidate property that follows.

03 — Sourcing

This is the part that happens out of view: working our network of vendors, agents and developers, filtering everything against your specific brief, and discarding anything that doesn't clear the bar before it ever reaches you. You'll typically see a handful of live opportunities, not a long list to wade through yourself.

04 — Deal Pack

Every opportunity that reaches you arrives with the numbers already done — comparable sales, a refurbishment estimate where relevant, projected income under more than one scenario, and a clear view on the exit. The aim is that your decision is "is this the right deal for me", not "can I even trust these figures."

05 — Completion

We coordinate solicitors, brokers, surveyors and contractors through to keys — and because we don't take ongoing management fees, our incentive at this stage is simply to get you to a clean completion, not to manufacture more billable work afterwards.
